# FixAm 9ja mailbox routing

| Mailbox | Public purpose | Recommended access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| info@fixam9ja.com | General information, partnerships and authentication sender | Information/support team |
| support@fixam9ja.com | Sign-in, technical support, account access and safety | Customer operations |
| customers@fixam9ja.com | Customer quotes, jobs, reviews and disputes | Customer operations |
| artisans@fixam9ja.com | Applications, onboarding, profiles and leads | Artisan operations |
| verification@fixam9ja.com | NIN/liveness and verification cases | Authorised verification team only |
| payments@fixam9ja.com | Subscriptions, payment confirmation and refunds | Finance/authorised operations |
| privacy@fixam9ja.com | Data rights, retention and account deletion | Privacy lead/authorised management |
| admin@fixam9ja.com | Internal administration and platform/store ownership | Directors and designated administrators only |

## Operating rules

- Enable MFA for every person with mailbox access.
- Grant shared-mailbox access to named users; never share one password.
- Do not request passwords, complete NINs, card PINs or authentication codes by email.
- Use categories or folders for new, awaiting-user, escalated and resolved cases.
- Restrict privacy, verification and payment mailboxes to staff who need that information.
- Maintain automatic replies confirming receipt and expected response time without exposing private case data.
- Review mailbox membership and forwarding rules regularly.
