FixAm 9ja marketplace policies.

These launch policies explain how customers, artisans, reviews, media, subscriptions, verification, and enforcement should work on the platform.

1. Platform purpose

FixAm 9ja helps customers find local artisans, request quotes, review work, and compare verified marketplace signals. Artisans use the platform to create profiles, receive quote leads, upload proof of work, and grow local visibility.

2. Customer policy

Customers should provide accurate job information, location, urgency, contact details, and relevant media. Customers must not submit fake requests, abusive messages, illegal jobs, or private information belonging to another person.

3. Artisan policy

Artisans must provide accurate profile details, trade, area, phone number, experience, work summary, and portfolio media. Artisans should respond to quote leads honestly and should not misrepresent skills, identity, location, availability, or pricing.

4. Verification policy

FixAm 9ja may request NIN, selfie/liveness checks, phone checks, portfolio review, and other trust checks before displaying badges or approving higher visibility. Verification improves trust but does not remove the need for customer judgement.

5. Reviews and ratings policy

Reviews should be written by customers who requested or completed work. FixAm 9ja does not edit honest reviews for image management. Content may be removed if it is fake, abusive, threatening, discriminatory, irrelevant, or exposes private information.

6. Media upload policy

Photos and videos should relate to the job, artisan portfolio, quote request, or completed work. Users must not upload private documents, harmful content, unrelated advertising, or media they do not have permission to share.

7. Subscription and founding artisan policy

During the founding stage, FixAm 9ja may give early verified artisans free or discounted launch visibility. Paid subscriptions may later unlock quote access, featured placement, analytics, and stronger local visibility.

8. Safety and enforcement policy

FixAm 9ja may warn, restrict, suspend, or remove users who misuse the platform, receive serious safety complaints, upload harmful content, manipulate reviews, or create trust risks.

9. Privacy and data notice

FixAm 9ja collects information needed to run the marketplace, including account details, quote requests, media uploads, profile details, reviews, and verification-related data. Sensitive information should be protected and used only for legitimate marketplace trust, safety, and operational purposes.

10. Contact and support

Users should contact FixAm 9ja support for account issues, suspicious activity, review concerns, verification questions, or platform access problems.
